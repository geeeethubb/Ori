**Executive pitch — 58 words.** Personal AI Playground is a working label for a personal AI that turns interests and everyday needs into useful apps made for you. Save a guitar inspiration, describe what you want to do, preview a practice app, make it yours, and return when you are ready. The Agent proposes; the Playground lets you choose; My apps keeps what proves useful.

**One-line promise:** Turn “I saved this for later” into “I can use this tonight.”

**Decision:** Demonstrate one complete guitar journey, not a broad claim of automated app-making. ## Consumer problem and first-use wedge

**Hypothesis, not user evidence:** Curious, AI-comfortable students and early-career builders collect inspirations but struggle to turn them into action. Start with a small cohort reached through campus communities. The narrow first use is a saved guitar inspiration plus the explicit need, “Help me practise tonight.” Test whether a tailored practice tool makes that next step easier than leaving the inspiration in a collection.

No real-user interviews, first-use observations, retention data, or market-size evidence were supplied. The wedge and audience are choices to test, not validated demand.

## Product and technology story

**Source or text note → grounded useful direction → real preview and one customization → user-selected usable app → save and reopen.** The Agent distinguishes what the user said from what a source actually contains. A link to an unreadable video can be saved, but its contents cannot be inferred; displaying an upload does not establish image analysis. Exploration without creation and **save-only** are legitimate outcomes. Saving does not silently create a permanent interest. Route to a suitable owned app **before** proposing another.  `use_existing_app` opens an action in the current app without changing its design;  `update_app_design` previews a separate spec revision. Proposal, preview, and cancel must not write data. Selection requires fresh ownership, context, revision, and capability checks. Designs use validated semantic view bindings and allowlisted actions—not generated executable code. Preserve existing accounts, records, app URLs, field IDs, revisions, and install metadata. New fields on old records must be nullable, version-gated, or backed by a verified safe default. Design undo must preserve later data; record-status undo is a distinct operation. ## Business model and first pilot

**Selected pricing experiment, not a billing claim:** 14 days of full feature access; **$7.50/month for the next two months; then $15/month**. Explain the included generation and revision allowance before asking for payment; do not call usage “unlimited” without a cost-backed limit. For each proposed allowance, test contribution margin as **price − payment/support costs − (expected new generations × cost per generation) − (expected revisions × cost per revision) − other variable serving costs**. Estimate the distributions and upper-cost cases from measured use and provider bills; no cost figures or billing implementation have been supplied.

**Immediate study:** Observe **5 first-use participants**, recruited from the hypothesized audience, attempting the guitar-to-action task without coaching. Capture what they saved, whether the direction was understood, whether they chose creation or reuse, their first meaningful action, confusion, and failures. This is diagnostic observation—not statistical validation or market evidence.

| Measure | Numerator / denominator | Counting rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Capture success** | Participants who intentionally save a usable note/source ÷ participants eligible and attempting capture | Saved-only counts; report failed attempts and actual denominator. |
| **Useful-action activation** | Participants who complete an exercise, timed practice action, or follow-up task action ÷ participants eligible and attempting the first-use task | Creating/opening an app or saving a link alone does **not** count. Report separately from capture success. |
| **Next-day passive reopen** | Activated participants who reopen on a later calendar day ÷ activated participants eligible for next-day observation | A reopen is not active reuse. |
| **Next-day active reuse** | Activated participants who perform another meaningful action on a later calendar day ÷ activated participants eligible for next-day observation | Separate prompted from unprompted returns; **do not** label a prompted return organic retention. |

## Five riskiest assumptions and cheapest checks

**Proposed thresholds below are qualitative go/no-go cues for a five-person study, not significance tests or market proof.** Record the actual eligible denominator and reasons for exclusions.

| Assumption | Cheapest real validation experiment | Proposed pass / stop cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saving inspiration leads to a usable first direction | Watch 5 participants capture their own or a realistic guitar need; ask what the Agent relied on. | **Pass:** at least 4/5 eligible participants can distinguish their note from source evidence; **stop/rework:** fabricated source-content claims or repeated confusion. |
| Preview makes the app feel worth creating | Have eligible participants inspect and customize Riff before choosing, without facilitator persuasion. | **Pass:** at least 3/5 eligible participants can name the customization and intended action; **stop/rework:** preview is mistaken for a completed app. |
| The first session produces real action | Observe an actual short practice start, pause, note, and confirmed save. | **Pass:** at least 3/5 eligible participants complete a meaningful practice action; **stop/rework:** they only create or open Riff. |
| Collection and reuse beat duplicate creation | Give a second, similar guitar need after the first app exists. | **Pass:** at least 3/5 eligible participants find and use existing Riff without a duplicate; **stop/rework:** duplicate proposals dominate. |
| Pricing and generation limits feel fair and feasible | Show the exact offer and a clearly stated proposed allowance; gather comprehension and instrument actual generation/revision cost. | **Pass:** at least 4/5 eligible participants can explain when the price changes and what is limited, **and** measured cost supports the allowance; **stop/rework:** confusion or unaffordable upper-cost usage. |
