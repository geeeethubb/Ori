# Ori visual review

This note maps each of the ten reference images in [`references/`](references/) to the screens that implement it and records what still differs. It is the visual-review item of the handoff in [`ORI_CLAUDE_BUILD_PROMPT.md`](ORI_CLAUDE_BUILD_PROMPT.md).

**What was compared.** The mockups were compared with screenshots of the real app in [`screens/`](screens/), taken by `scripts/screenshots.mjs` on 15 November 2026 against a throwaway server in Sample mode (scripted replies, no live model), after the last product-code commit. `<screen>-390.jpg` is a 390 px phone viewport (Chromium with an iPhone user agent), `<screen>-1440.jpg` is desktop, and `-view` files show only the first viewport. The run also rendered 320 and 768 px; those files are not kept here. Example apps, ideas and preview content come from Sample mode and licensed fixture photos ([`../ASSET_SOURCES.md`](../ASSET_SOURCES.md)) and are labelled Example, Example profile or Sample mode in the product; the notes and photo "shared" in the Light journey are scripted inputs.

**Reading the captures.** In tall full-page captures the fixed phone tab bar lands mid-page, and a dialog's dimmed backdrop covers only the first desktop viewport; both come from the full-page capture, not the layout. The shared photo is the fixture `light-1.jpg`, so the same picture shows as "Your photo" and as an Example tile. The captures in [`screens/states/`](screens/states/) are older than the main set (see [States](#states)). For a before and after, the pre-Ori redesign was captured under the same file names in [`../screenshots/after/`](../screenshots/after/) (the folder name refers to that earlier redesign).

**Rule for conflicts.** Where the mockups disagree with each other, the written spec [`ORI_DESIGN_SPEC.md`](ORI_DESIGN_SPEC.md) wins. "Rule N" below is discrepancy rule N in its section 1: (1) one canonical icon per app, (2) no parent navigation inside apps, (3) no pretend playback, (4) build stages only from real job states, (5) no bell or menu without a destination, (6) label example data, (7) ink text on orange, (8) Done is not proof of installation, (9) honest success copy, (10) pocket art only on marketing or empty states. Differences are marked **Spec** (the spec requires it), **Choice** (a design decision the spec allows) or **Open** (a gap, collected in [Open differences](#open-differences)).

- **Known limit,** stated on the gallery page itself: on lilac the Making ring (about 1.5:1) and the lime dots (about 1.3:1) are faint, so a text label always carries the state; cobalt on ink is about 2.5:1, so dark apps seat the companion on a lilac disc, as Riff's pill does. Motion cannot be judged from stills.

## Platforms
- **Phone and desktop:** every main screen has a 390 and a 1440 capture, except three phone-only views (the two whole previews and the Android install sheet); most states are 390 only. The phone captures are browser emulation, not a phone.
- **Android emulator** ([`evidence/android/`](evidence/android/); headless Pixel 9 Pro image, Android 17, the Ori shell APK against a local Sample-mode server, 15 November; details in [`../ANDROID.md`](../ANDROID.md)): [cold start](evidence/android/01-cold-start-landing.jpg) on the landing, [sign-in](evidence/android/02-sign-in.jpg), the [signed-in Playground](evidence/android/03-signed-in-playground.jpg), and [the shell's launcher shortcut intent opening Light directly](evidence/android/04-shortcut-intent-cold-light.jpg) from cold: Light with its saved photo and caption and no Ori navigation. This is one shell with per-app shortcuts, not a separate APK per app. In the cold-start frame the landing's Light example photo is still a blank panel; no later frame was captured.
- **Not verified:** no physical phone was available, so adding an app to an iPhone or Android home screen on a real device, and opening it from that icon, is unverified. On the emulator, tapping a pinned icon for a cold launch is still blocked by Play Protect on the account-less image, and pinning a new shortcut was not re-run. No screen-reader pass was done.

## Open differences
1. **The state captures are older than the product.** Recapture `screens/states/` from the final build; they show the older header, Light tagline and tabs, Riff glyph, "screenshot" wording and Make mine bar listed under [States](#states).
2. **Two reference moments have no final capture:** the pending Make screen for Light (04) and 07's "Keep going" (success toast with Undo, Camera / Shutter / Aperture in Light's photo form).
3. **Agent at 390 (02):** after a photo share the first screen starts at the reply; the person's note and most of the photo are above the fold.
4. **Playground with the seeded profile (03):** two look-alike board sketches with truncated column labels, plainer than 03's photo, timer and queue cards.
5. **Copy:** idea titles say "Practise in Riff" (British spelling of the verb), where board A writes "Practice guitar, your way."
6. **Android cold start:** the landing's Light example photo had not appeared in the captured frame.
7. **Unverified:** adding to and launching from a real iPhone or Android home screen, the Android pinned-icon tap launch, and a screen-reader pass.
