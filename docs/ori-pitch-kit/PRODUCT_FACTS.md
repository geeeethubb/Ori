# Ori product facts for the pitch

What the 16-slide revision may claim about Ori, read from the code on 2026-11-15 (product code at HEAD `b8a1808`). This is a reading of the code and the existing evidence, not a new test run. It serves `ORI_PITCH_REVISION_BRIEF.md`. **SHIPPED** = in the product, with `path:line` evidence from the repository root. **PARTIAL** = exactly the part that exists. **NOT SHIPPED** = vision only; show it only as "Concept" or "Ori vision".

Two modes matter for every claim. The hosted site runs **live AI** (Claude) and its sign-up works (`docs/ori/evidence/render-live-check-2026-11-15.txt:6,9`). The three pitch recordings were made in **Sample mode**: scripted, no model (section 8).

## 1. What a person can share (slides 3, 5, 6)

- **SHIPPED: a typed thought**, up to 4,000 characters, in the Agent composer (`server/routes/agent.ts:168-177`). The first screen says "A thought. A photo. A link." (`client/src/pages/AgentPage.tsx:492`).
- **SHIPPED: a photo** (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 6 MB, served only to its owner): `client/src/pages/AgentPage.tsx:662-663`, `server/repos/media.ts:35-41`, `server/config.ts:30`, `server/routes/media.ts:1-3`. Live AI looks at it (`server/routes/agent.ts:86-104`, `server/model/anthropic.ts:701`; live: `render-live-check-2026-11-15.txt:33-35`). Sample mode saves it unread and says so (`server/model/sample/respond.ts:585`, `shared/context.ts:100-104`).
- **SHIPPED: a link, saved but never fetched** (`server/services/linkMeta.ts:1-5`; composer hint "Paste a link. Ori saves it but never reads the page.", `AgentPage.tsx:613`). YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud and Spotify links get only their public title, author and thumbnail via oEmbed, shown as "Title read, content not read" (`linkMeta.ts:16-21,84`; `shared/context.ts:92-97`). Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, Threads and Pinterest links are saved and never read; no other page is read either (`linkMeta.ts:23-30,59-68`).
- **A reel is therefore a saved link.** Ori works from the person's note or screenshot and says what it did not read (`server/model/sample/respond.ts:87`, `server/model/anthropic.ts:705`). From a share, memory is learned only from the person's own words (`server/routes/agent.ts:133-141`), though a fact taken from the note can carry the share's label, e.g. "from Instagram link" (`docs/ori/screens/memory-1440.jpg`).
- **NOT SHIPPED: sharing from Instagram's (or any app's) share menu.** The Android shell registers only a launcher intent (`android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:21-24`) and no web manifest has a share target (`server/install.ts:124-143`). People copy the link and paste it.
- **SHIPPED: a file, in Bring context only**: .txt, .md or .json, up to 200 KB (`client/src/pages/parts/ImportParts.tsx:171-181`, `shared/context.ts:12`). The Agent takes images only: no PDFs or documents.
- **SHIPPED: a pasted ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini summary**, up to 60,000 characters (`ImportParts.tsx:47-48,68-107`; `server/routes/context.ts:27-45`; `shared/context.ts:10`).
- **NOT SHIPPED: reading any account** (ChatGPT, Claude, Instagram, Spotify, Google). The product says "Only what you add here is used. Nothing is read from your accounts or other chats." (`client/src/pages/ImportPage.tsx:84`; also `client/src/pages/PlaygroundPage.tsx:183`) and answers sync requests with "There is no connection to … here" (`server/model/sample/unsupported.ts:40`). There is no OAuth or connector dependency (`package.json:23-39`); the only outbound calls are the model API and those oEmbed title lookups (`linkMeta.ts:1-5`).
- Slide 5's four inputs are all real as things the person brings: a pasted AI summary, a reel link (saved, not read), a photo (read only by live AI), a typed thought. Never draw them as sync connectors. Say: "A thought. A photo. A link." / "Paste what your AI already knows." Don't: "Ori reads your Instagram / ChatGPT / Claude."

## 2. Bring context from your AI (slide 8A)

SHIPPED at `/settings/import` (`client/src/pages/ImportPage.tsx:1-5,76-114`), linked from the Agent's first screen (`AgentPage.tsx:507-511`) and from What you've shared (`client/src/pages/MemoryPage.tsx:221`).

1. **Copy prompt**: "Copy the prompt" copies a fixed export prompt (`ImportParts.tsx:21-58`). It asks the AI to use only what it may, mark facts Confirmed or Inferred, leave out secrets and other people's private details, and end with items to review (`client/src/pages/parts/exportPrompt.ts:5-32`).
2. **Ask your AI**, outside Ori: "Copy the prompt, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any assistant you use, review the answer, then paste it below." (`ImportParts.tsx:47-48`)
3. **Bring back the summary**: Paste tab, "The profile your AI wrote", optional "Where it came from", **Add it** (`ImportParts.tsx:68-107`). Alternatives: Upload a file, or answer four short Questions (`ImportPage.tsx:24-29`; `ImportParts.tsx:206-242`).
4. **Ori reads it**: facts are extracted (live model or Sample rules), merged with existing ones, contradictions flagged, then saved (`server/services/contextImport.ts:17-55`).
5. **Review what is remembered**: the page lands on "What you've shared" (`ImportPage.tsx:41-50`): Interests, Current needs, Routines & tools, Recent changes, Open questions; each fact marked Confirmed or Inferred with its source; edit, remove, or "Keep this" for a contradiction; removing a source removes what was learned only from it (`MemoryPage.tsx:1-7,149-211`; `server/routes/context.ts:59-99`).

- Precision: the review inside Ori comes **after** Add it (facts are saved, then edited or removed); the review before sharing is of the AI's answer, outside Ori. Labelled example profiles exist (`ImportParts.tsx:246-265`).
- Real screens (Sample mode): `docs/ori/screens/import-390.jpg`, `docs/ori/screens/memory-1440.jpg`.
- Say: "Copy prompt → Ask your AI → Bring back the summary → Review what Ori keeps." Provider names only as where the text comes from. Don't: "Connect ChatGPT", "import your Claude history".

## 9. Competitor capability notes (brief section 6, restated; not re-researched)

- Checked by the brief on 2026-11-15 (`ORI_PITCH_REVISION_BRIEF.md:468-475`). Map placements are qualitative judgments of product focus, not published scores.
- **Claude (artifacts)** documents interactive tools, AI-powered apps, persistent storage and connected apps. Don't claim Ori is unique because its apps save data or use AI.
- **Lovable** documents building, iterating and deploying apps, including backend and database. It is not a static mockup generator; it revises apps.
- **Notion** has AI connectors and agent memories, so it has context and memory. Don't call broad workspaces context-free.
- Never claim competitors only chat, forget everything, cannot store data or cannot build apps. Ori's proposed advantage is the integrated, phone-first personal journey and lower friction for the target user, not exclusive primitives (`ORI_PITCH_REVISION_BRIEF.md:261`).
- Map (`ORI_PITCH_REVISION_BRIEF.md:236-259`): X "Starting point", from "An app you already know you need" to "Your context, interests, and unclear ideas"; Y "Everyday experience", from "A general workspace" to "Distinct personal apps you return to"; label "Product positioning"; no numeric scores; overlap with Claude is allowed.
- Ori's verified position (sections 1-7): starts from shared notes, photos, links and pasted summaries; makes distinct apps with their own link, icon and manifest; changes are reviewed by the person and keep records. Show only as "Ori vision" (outlined or dashed): reading accounts, share-menu capture, background adaptation, verified phone home screens or per-app installs, reminders, external automation.
